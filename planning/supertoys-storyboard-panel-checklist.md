## Camera Move Legend
- `Locked`: No motion, emotional stillness.
- `Drift`: Slow slider/dolly for dreamlike tone.
- `Orbit`: Reserved for emotional pivot moments.
- `Handheld-lite`: Minimal life in child POV shots.

- David should shift from polished certainty -> private vulnerability.
